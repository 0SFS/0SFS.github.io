0SFS — Open Source Flight Simulator

A real-world browser flight simulator. Nothing to install, no account.

0SFS runs the JSBSim flight dynamics model in WebAssembly, over real-world scenery streamed as you fly: free maps draped over elevation data, or Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles with your own key. It is free software under the AGPL-3.0, and the source is on GitHub.
