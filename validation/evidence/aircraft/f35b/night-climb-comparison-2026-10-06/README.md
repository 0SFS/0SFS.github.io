# Night climb reference

Selected original-size decoded frames from the user-provided
[F-35B night air-show video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JX-loX8NGm0).
They support qualitative comparison with the existing carrier-landing reference.
See [provenance.json](provenance.json) for publisher metadata, timestamps,
source/frame hashes and limitations. The frames have no exposure or color edits.

The full video is working material in the ignored build tree, not distributed
evidence. The source remains credited to spencerhughes2255; no public-domain or
asset-reuse license is asserted. These excerpts are retained for technical review
on the machine that decoded them and are gitignored, not distributed;
provenance.json has the timestamps and hashes to decode and verify them again.
