    Report wheel spin relative to the airframe
    
    gear/unit[i]/wheel-spin-rad_sec now reads and writes the wheel spin rate
    relative to the airframe, positive rolling forward, through a getter/setter
    tie. The spin state and its integration, ground initialization, solver
    Jacobians and tread slip keep their meaning: the state is measured in the same
    frame as the airframe rates in in.PQR (FGPropagate::GetPQR(), relative to the
    ECEF frame), and the property subtracts the airframe rate about the spin axis.
    
    In ground contact the spin axis is the brake row's, ground normal cross the
    rolling direction. In the air it is gear up cross the steered wheel forward
    direction, from the current gear orientation and steering angle.
    
    Bearing and brake drag act between wheel and airframe, so the airborne drag
    now damps the relative spin: a braked wheel stays at rest relative to a
    rotating airframe instead of stopping in the ground frame. No damping happens
    during trim or when the time step is not positive.
    
    TestWheelSpin reconstructs the spin state from the relative rate for its
    momentum estimate, computes the airborne spin-down in the right frame, and
    adds regressions for the property transform, RunIC and reset, the airborne
    drag of both spin signs, a braked wheel on a pitching airframe, and the
    airborne axle of a steered wheel.
