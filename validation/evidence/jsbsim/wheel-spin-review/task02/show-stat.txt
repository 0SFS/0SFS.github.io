    Configure wheel friction bounds before clamping
    
    ConfigureWheelSpinRows now sets the wheel roll row's tire-grip bound with
    the rest of that row, and the ordinary BOGEY path keeps only its braking
    bound. The configuration runs before the roll and side warm-start values
    are clamped, so each clamp sees its row's final limits. The rows are still
    registered roll, side, then wheel brake, and the brake bound is unchanged.
    
    In ConfigureWheelSpinRows, roll and axle are renamed rollDirection and
    axleLeverArm: the axle term is the lever arm from the CG to the axle.
