    Test wheel constraints without Python
    
    FGAccelerationsTest drives the friction solve through the public inputs of
    FGAccelerations with synthetic Lagrange multiplier rows, so that the CxxTest
    coverage run (BUILD_PYTHON_MODULE=OFF, ctest -R Test1) exercises the wheel
    spin coupling. Each case uses a fresh FGFDMExec, identity frames, a 2 slug
    airframe with J = diag(4, 8, 16), a wheel of 1 ft radius and 1 slug*ft^2
    spin inertia, and a 0.1 s step. The results are compared with values
    derived by hand:
    
    - a friction row without a wheel (lambda = -32);
    - a free wheel, which rolls without slipping after the step while the
      airframe receives only the moment of the force at the axle;
    - a brake between the wheel and the airframe, which stops the relative spin
      and conserves the angular momentum;
    - rolling and braking rows on one wheel, whose -1 off-diagonal term through
      the shared wheel is needed for both constraints to hold (lambda = -360/17
      and -280/17);
    - a brake limited to +/-2 from a warm start outside its bounds;
    - rows on different wheels, which the wheels do not couple;
    - a zero time step, which applies no friction and resets the wheel
      acceleration, and an empty multiplier list, which clears the friction.
    
    Tests only.
