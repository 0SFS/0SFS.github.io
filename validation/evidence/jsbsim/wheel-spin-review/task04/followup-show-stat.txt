    Clarify wheel spin limits and axis alignment
    
    The FGLGear class documentation separated two airborne conditions that it
    had conflated. On a positive-time airborne step outside trim, damping stops
    the wheel relative to the airframe only if the decrement is at least the
    magnitude of the relative spin before damping. Once stopped, the wheel stays
    stopped only while the decrement absorbs the magnitude of later changes in
    the airframe rate projected on the current axle. The approximation does not
    enforce a rigid brake lock.
    
    The ground and airborne spin axes coincide when the ground normal lies in
    the plane of gear up and steered forward and has a positive component along
    gear up. Coplanarity alone would also admit a reversed normal, which
    reverses the ground axis.
    
    Documentation only.
