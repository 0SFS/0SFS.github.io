    Document wheel friction and airborne spin-down
    
    The airborne spin-down names its constants legacyTreadDeceleration
    (13 ft/s^2, copied from the legacy wheel-speed-fps spin-down) and
    additionalBrakeTreadDeceleration (100 ft/s^2, an uncalibrated approximation
    introduced with the wheel spin DOF, neither a measured brake torque nor
    derived from BrakeFCoeff). The decrement keeps the same arithmetic order and
    the legacy spin-down is unchanged.
    
    The FGLGear class documentation now contrasts the two meanings of
    rolling_friction: tire rolling resistance without the wheel elements, and
    part of the wheel-to-axle torque, bounded by BrakeFCoeff times the normal
    load times the radius, with them. It covers the opt-in elements and their
    units, the tread force bound and axle lever arm, the no-slip and
    no-relative-spin conditions, ground initialization, the airborne
    approximation and its limits, the ground and airborne spin axes and the jump
    they can cause in the reported rate at a contact transition, and the frame
    of the spin properties. It no longer says aircraft without the elements are
    unchanged: their friction rows use the reordered solver arithmetic.
    
    The comment above ConfigureWheelSpinRows gives the Jacobians and velocity
    conditions of both rows, says the airframe lever arm runs from the CG to the
    axle, and links the derivation at
    https://github.com/JSBSim-Team/jsbsim/pull/1502#issuecomment-5654721219.
    
    No behavior changes.
