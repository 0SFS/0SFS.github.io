Compressor stall and its recovery, F-16 at 20,000 ft and 300 kt CAS.

Stall() is selected by the Stalled flag ahead of every other phase, burns
IdleFF and returns zero thrust, and is cleared only by a throttle below
1 %. The engine then re-enters tpRun at whatever N2 the stall left, and
Run() seeks N2 to the idle target, so the member N2norm returns to 0 from
above rather than going below it. The idle thrust is negative here because
the F-16 IdleThrust table models ram drag at speed; that is the model, not
a defect. Whether tpRun can be entered with N2 below IdleN2, which would
make the unbounded N2norm negative and the N2norm^2 thrust term grow again
as the spool falls, is NOT demonstrated by this run.
