# Commercial use and licensing

OSFS original source code is offered under AGPL-3.0-only. AGPLv3 permits commercial use, including selling copies and providing commercial services, subject to its terms. This document does not add a condition, fee, revenue share, or approval requirement to AGPL-licensed code.

Hobby, education, research, and open-source work are welcome. Organizations that generate more than **$1,000 USD per month** from products or services based on OSFS are invited to contact the project maintainers through the repository before launch. The purpose is a conversation, not a mandatory license gate.

Possible voluntary or separately negotiated arrangements include sponsorship, support agreements, commercial licensing from the relevant copyright holders, partnership agreements, or revenue sharing. Any revenue-share arrangement is negotiated case by case; a possible range is 0–10%, with no mandatory percentage.

Only copyright holders with authority over the relevant code can offer a separate commercial license. Third-party dependencies, aircraft data, models, and service/data providers retain their own terms. A commercial arrangement does not waive their notices, source obligations, attribution, API rules, or asset permissions.

This policy must remain separate from the AGPL text. Adding a non-permissive commercial condition to distributed AGPL code would conflict with AGPLv3's prohibition on further restrictions.
